Research

Thesis work on dynamic routing, plus a supply chain model in preparation.

M.Sc. thesis · Proposal defended · Advisor: Prof. Majid Salari · 2025 to present

A reinforcement learning framework for intervention timing and level in route re-optimization

Dynamic Pickup and Delivery Problem with Time Windows (DPDPTW)

  • Frames adaptive routing intervention as a sequential decision problem under uncertainty, with operational constraints and real-time limits.
  • Learns policies that decide when to re-optimize and how strongly to intervene as requests arrive.
  • Tests the policies in computational experiments against alternative routing strategies.
  • Service feasibility
  • Travel cost
  • Fleet utilization
  • Responsiveness
  • Route stability
  • Computational effort
Manuscript in preparation · 2026

Integrated poultry supply chain optimization

With Dr. Hossein Neghabi

  • Mixed-integer model covering farm selection, production, slaughterhouses, meat distribution and pet food production.
  • Recovers poultry mortality and slaughterhouse by-products for pet food, with material balances, yields and capacities.
  • Minimizes total cost across upstream and downstream flows, including direct and distribution-center delivery.
Accepted and presented · ICISE 2026

Combination meal design with market basket analysis

Nokar, Khani, Koosha & Madani-Talab · 12th International Conference on Industrial and Systems Engineering

  • Analyzed 600,000+ POS orders (about 1.4 million item records) across 87 menu items.
  • Found 1,448 frequent itemsets and 589 association rules, with Apriori and FP-Growth agreeing exactly.
  • Extended basket analysis with a Product Attractiveness Rate.

Projects

Family Capacitated Vehicle Routing (F-CVRP)

Hybrid metaheuristic · Ferdowsi University of Mashhad
  • Built FRTS-SP, combining randomized family-aware construction, local search, Reactive Tabu Search, route-pool generation and set-partitioning recombination.
  • Evaluated 384 benchmark instances, with a 25.23% mean cost reduction over the construction stage.
  • Added strict feasibility checks, parameter calibration and a five-seed robustness study on 48 instances.

Python · SciPy · Jupyter Notebook

Work and time study of a dishwasher assembly line

Course project · Jahan Afrooz Manufacturing and Industrial Company
  • Used repeated stopwatch observations, performance rating, allowances, two-hand analysis, MTM, MOST, assembly mapping and precedence analysis.
  • Identified the test room as the main bottleneck and proposed layout, workstation and line-balancing changes.

Facility planning for 70-watt street-light production

Course project · Ferdowsi University of Mashhad
  • Planned the full facility: product and process analysis, AHP and SWOT location evaluation, capacity, material flow, space, layout, transport and warehouse design.
  • Generated layouts with from-to analysis, activity relationships, the spiral method, ALDEP and CRAFT.
  • Estimated about 6,000 units a year and 1,732 m² of required area under the project assumptions.

Feasibility study for cosmetic cream manufacturing

Team project · Ferdowsi University of Mashhad
  • Assessed market, technical, operational, facility and financial feasibility, from demand and supply to process design.
  • Evaluated investment with ROI, NPV, payback and sensitivity analysis.
  • Compared financing and working-capital scenarios over a multi-year horizon.
